Car & start line

Drag the orange car along the road to check the scale.

Drag the checkered start/finish line to move it. Bridges you have stay as they are.

Click the blue arrow past the line to race the other way. The Blender path, the start marker and Drive follow; bridges stay as they are.

Drive (P): Rocket League car physics, Drift Tower rules

You start on your roof at the start line. Hold Boost and steer to turtle-drift. On the roof steering turns the car to its right, so it looks reversed, like in RL. Throttle and jump roll you back onto your wheels, like in RL.

Keyboard + mouse: W/S throttle (and pitch) · A/D steer · left mouse or K boost · right mouse or Space jump · Shift powerslide / air roll · Q/E air roll · scroll zoom

Controller: RT / LT throttle · left stick steer and pitch · B boost · A jump · X powerslide / air roll · LB / RB air roll · right stick zoom · View reset · Menu restart

R back to the last checkpoint · Enter restart from the line · Esc stop driving. A wall touch, or 0.4 s on your wheels, puts you back at the last checkpoint (switch these off in the corner).

Credits: car physics: RocketSim by ZealanL (MIT) with Bullet Physics (zlib). Octane and Fennec models: BrokenHypocrite's rlcars mod for Minecraft (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), which also showed RocketSim could drive this. Not made or endorsed by Psyonix.

Blender

Paste into Blender's Text Editor and press Run. It builds a path at the 3D cursor (same axes as here) and a road with Array + Curve that closes the loop exactly, at the widths and heights you see. No road piece by that name in the file? It builds one like track 1's.
